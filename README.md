## 🌟 Key Architectural Features

### 1. ⏱️ The Signature Circular Counter Loader
- Counts up from `0%` to `100%` in even numbers (`0%`, `2%`, `4%` ... `100%`).
- Synchronized SVG perimeter circle stroke tracking completion.
- Smooth curtain wipe sweeping upward into the viewport upon load.

### 2. 🔤 Silicon Valley Typography Stack
- **Display Headings**: [**Plus Jakarta Sans**](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans) `(600, 700, 800)` — Geometric, modern, and authoritative.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: [**Inter**](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) `(400, 500, line-height: 1.65)` — Extreme clarity and zero eye fatigue.
- **Data & Indices**: [**JetBrains Mono**](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono) — Developer-first monospace for index tags (`01`, `02`), years `( 2026 )`, and metrics.
- **Clean Palette**: Minimalist slate white canvas (`#f8fafc`) with deep charcoal (`#0f172a`), slate body (`#475569`), and tech blue (`#2563eb`) accents.

### 3. ✦ Authentic 8-Point Geometric Star Accents
- The exact geometric star SVG vector from `joegarnerdesign.com` rotating smoothly on section headers (`✦ Featured ✦`, `✦ FAQ ✦`, and navigation badges).
